# Prompts for segments 9-19 — "אין ייאוש בעולם"

Each segment is a 10-second window of the song (segment N = song second (N-1)*10 to N*10;
segment 19 is 180 -> 187.6 s). Cut the song windows exactly like segments 1-8.
Timings below come from the Suno lyric alignment of the song.

Song map: intro 0-13 instrumental | whispered intro 13-33 | instrumental 33-52 | verse 1 rap 52.6-65.7 |
chant 66.5-78.4 | chorus 81-91.6 | instrumental 91.6-108.5 | verse 2 108.5-115.6 | build-up 115.8-122.1 |
final chorus 122.7-136.6 | outro 136.6-142.4 | instrumental 142-164 | "אני כאן" 163.8-169 | instrumental to 187.6.

## Shared blocks (add to every prompt)

**STYLE**: cinematic 16:9, ultra-detailed 4K, rich saturated golden-hour and violet-purple light, volumetric haze,
painterly-photoreal look, smooth stable camera, 24 fps — same look as the earlier clips (Jerusalem at night, the
glowing portal, the canyon at sunset). No text, no subtitles, no logos, no extra people.

**CHARACTER** (whenever he appears): the same man as in the previous clips — about 40, short dark beard, round
glasses, grey-and-white knitted kippah, black jacket over a grey hoodie, black over-ear headphones around his
neck, small Star of David pendant.

Times inside each prompt are seconds from the start of that 10 s clip. The mouth should move only where marked.

## 9 · 80-90 s · Chorus opens — "איי איי איי… תן לי לעוף…" (sung ~1-4.7 s and ~8-10 s)
Medium shot of the man on the canyon cliff edge at sunset, golden light with soft violet haze (continues the
end of segment 8). 0-1 s: he stands with eyes closed, a breeze moving his jacket. 1-4.7 s: he sings melodically
toward the sky, chest opening, arms slowly spreading wide. 4.7-8 s: silence, shimmering golden-violet particles
rise around him and his boots barely lift off the rock. 8-10 s: he sings again with a joyful smile, head tilted
up. Camera: slow push-in, then a gentle upward tilt.

## 10 · 90-100 s · "…לראות את הטוב" then instrumental (sung only 0-1.6 s)
He rises gently from the cliff and floats above the vast canyon at sunset. 0-1.6 s: he finishes the sung line
with a smile, then closes his mouth and stays silent. 1.6-10 s: he floats upward with open arms and a peaceful
face. Camera pulls back and cranes up into a wide aerial view: a golden river winding through the canyon,
glowing clouds; he ends as a small figure in a luminous sky. Instrumental, mouth closed.

## 11 · 100-110 s · Instrumental, verse 2 starts at 8.5 s ("נופל וקם, שוב מתחיל")
Wide cinematic glide over Jerusalem's Old City at dusk — golden stone walls, glowing domes, lanterns, violet
mist. The man descends softly into a narrow stone alley lit by hanging lanterns and lands. 0-8 s: instrumental,
he walks calmly toward the camera, no speech. 8.5-10 s: he begins to rap quietly to the camera, low and
hypnotic. Camera: smooth aerial descent into a steady follow shot.

## 12 · 110-120 s · Verse 2 (0-5.6 s) + build-up "אור… אור… אור…" (5.8-10 s)
Medium close-up in the stone alley, minimal hypnotic rap. 0-5.6 s: he raps; on "falls and gets up again" he
stumbles, drops to one knee, then rises with determination (about 0.5-3 s). On "above time" (about 3-5 s)
glowing clock-like rings and star trails slowly rotate in the air behind him. 5.8-10 s: he whispers a mantra
"light… light… light…" (mouth moves softly) and a golden glow grows from his chest and open palms, brighter and
brighter. Camera: slow push-in.

## 13 · 120-130 s · "תן לי אמונה" then FINAL CHORUS "אין ייאוש בעולם!" (sung 0-2.1 s, 2.7-5.8 s, 7.8-9.5 s)
Build to the explosion. 0-2.1 s: he whispers the last words of the build-up, glow at maximum. At 2.7 s a burst
of golden-violet light explodes outward, the lanterns flare, psychedelic fractal energy rings ripple through the
stones. 2.7-10 s: he sings with full power, arms thrown wide, while the energy forms a column of light rising to
the sky. Camera: quick whip-zoom on the explosion, then a dynamic orbit.

## 14 · 130-140 s · Peak "אין ייאוש בעולם!… תאיר לי את העולם!" (sung 0-6.6 s), then calm
The camera cranes up past the Old City rooftops as a huge column of light rises into the night sky and blooms
into a radiant spiral galaxy of gold and violet. The man, small in the foreground on a rooftop terrace, sings
with full emotion for the first 6.6 s. At 6.6 s he lowers his arms, palms open to the sky, tears of joy and a
soft smile, mouth closed. 7-10 s: the light settles into floating golden particles. Do not show any other person.

## 15 · 140-150 s · Outro echoes (a single whispered "אור" at ~2 s, otherwise silent)
Calm outro: he sits on the ancient city wall above Jerusalem at night, three-quarters turned, eyes closed,
breathing slowly, with soft particles of light floating around him. At about 2 s he whispers one word,
"light". Camera: slow orbit around him, the moon rising over the domes, violet mist drifting. Peaceful,
echoing atmosphere.

## 16 · 150-160 s · Instrumental
No people (at most a tiny distant silhouette). A slow cinematic glide over Jerusalem at night: lit walls, golden
domes, cypress trees, violet mist, a full moon in a dramatic purple-clouded sky — matching the opening shot of
the video. The camera drifts toward the glowing stone archway with the hanging lantern.

## 17 · 160-170 s · "אני כאן…" (whispered ~3.8-9 s)
The man stands inside the lantern-lit stone arch (the opening location), looks into the camera with a warm calm
smile and gently reaches his open hand toward the camera — the same welcoming gesture as earlier. 0-3.8 s:
silent. 3.8-9 s: he softly whispers "I'm here…", slow and breathy. Then a smile.

## 18 · 170-180 s · Instrumental
He lowers his hand, turns and walks slowly away through the glowing archway into the alley, the lantern light
flickering. The camera slowly pulls back and rises to reveal the Jerusalem skyline under the full moon. Violet
mist swirls, lights twinkle. The figure becomes small and disappears into the warm glow. No singing.

## 19 · 180-187.6 s (7.6 s clip) · Final shot — closes the loop
Peaceful wide, almost static shot: Jerusalem at night, full moon in a purple stormy sky, golden city lights,
violet mist, the stone archway with a glowing lantern on the left, purple bougainvillea flowers. Only gentle
motion (drifting mist, twinkling lights). **The very last frame must match the first frame of segment 1**
(file `loop_end_frame_seg1_first.jpg`) so the video loops seamlessly: if your generator supports an end frame,
use that image as the end frame.
